Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

What this covers

This policy explains what UlamHub collects when you use the app, why, and how you can delete it. It applies to the mobile app and the account you create in it.

Account information

When you create an account, we (via Supabase, our authentication and database provider) store your email address and an encrypted password, plus any display name, handle, or avatar you set.

Content you create

Recipes you add or import, your meal plan, your grocery list, and any kitchen you create or join are stored on your device. If you're signed in, this same content is also synced to our cloud database so it's available across your devices. That includes a recipe's photo — one you pick yourself, or one UlamHub pulls automatically from an imported link's own post or video — which is uploaded to our cloud storage (via Supabase) alongside the rest of the recipe.

Kitchen crew sharing

If you join a kitchen, the other members can see your display name and browse your recipes. If you request to copy a crew member's recipe into your own cookbook, they see your name and the recipe you asked for so they can approve or decline it.

AI features

Kitchen AI, Leftover Alchemist, Ingredient Scanner, What Can I Make?, Party Planner, Recipe Story, and Support Chat send the text or photo you submit to OpenAI to generate a response. Separately, importing a recipe from a link sends that page's content to OpenAI so it can be read into a structured recipe. We require a signed-in session for these features so we can prevent abuse of this — we do not use your content to train our own models.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We don't share your content with anyone outside the service providers above (Supabase for hosting, OpenAI for AI features) needed to run the app.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and everything tied to it — recipes, meal plan, grocery list, and kitchen membership — at any time from Profile → Account → Delete account. This can't be undone.

Children

UlamHub is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data — including requests to delete or export it — can be sent to support@ulamhub.com.
