What is UlamHub

A recipe app that actually cooks with you

UlamHub started as a home for Filipino ulam — the everyday dishes that go with rice — and grew into a kitchen companion for whatever you're actually cooking. Save recipes from anywhere, lean on an AI sous-chef when you're stuck, and let UlamHub turn your week into a plan and your plan into a grocery list.

How to bring in a recipe from Instagram, TikTok or YouTube

Open Grocery List — ingredients from every day you added combine automatically. Tap Substitute on anything you can't find.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is UlamHub?+
UlamHub is a recipe app rooted in Filipino home cooking and built for every cuisine. You can save recipes from anywhere, get help from an AI sous-chef, plan your week, and shop with a grocery list that builds itself.
What cuisines can I cook with UlamHub?+
Filipino, Italian, American, Mexican, Japanese, Indian, Chinese, Greek, Thai, Korean, Vietnamese, French, Spanish and Lebanese are all built into Explore today — browse by cuisine or search for a specific dish or ingredient.
How do I add a recipe I found online or on social media?+
The fast way: right from the post or video, tap Share and choose UlamHub — the app opens straight into the import, link already in hand. Or do it manually: from Add Recipe, choose Import from link and paste a URL from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or a blog. Either way, AI reads the content and builds a clean recipe card you can review and save.
Can UlamHub read a photo of a handwritten recipe?+
Yes — choose Scan a photo from Add Recipe and AI reads a handwritten or printed recipe straight from the photo.
What can Kitchen AI actually do?+
Kitchen AI is a chat assistant inside the app — ask it what to cook with what you have, have it plan your week to a budget, or ask it a cooking question, by text or voice.
Does UlamHub help me use up leftovers?+
Yes — Leftover Alchemist takes a few photos of what's left in your fridge or pantry, an optional cuisine preference, and invents a dish built around it.
Does UlamHub build a grocery list for me?+
It builds the list you ask it to. Tap Add to grocery list on any day in your Meal Planner, and that day's ingredients merge in — the same ingredient from multiple days combines into one line instead of listing twice. Nothing gets added automatically. Each item has a Substitute button, and Cooking Abroad mode adds substitutes plus the nearest specialty store.
Can I cook with family or share my recipes with someone else?+
Yes — My Kitchen gives you an invite code to share. Anyone who joins with it can request access to browse and add your recipes, and you can request to join someone else's kitchen the same way.
Is UlamHub free?+
UlamHub is free to use for saving recipes, meal planning and your grocery list. Some AI-powered tools are part of UlamHub Premium.
Is UlamHub available on iOS and Android?+
UlamHub is built for both and is currently in final testing — see the Download section above for store availability.
