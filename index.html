How UlamHub works

From "What Do I Have" to "It's Done"

Three real steps — this is the actual flow in the app, not a simplified version of it.

1

Add it, review it, save it

Type a recipe in, paste a link, or snap a photo — AI fills in the ingredients, steps and nutrition. Fix anything it missed, then save it to your cookbook.

2

Plan the week, then shop for it

Auto-fill each day from your saved recipes, or fill it yourself. Tap Add to grocery list on the days you need — nothing's added automatically, and duplicate ingredients combine into one line.

3

Cook, hands-free

Start Cooking drops you into Cook Mode — one step at a time, full screen, with timers built in, so you're never hunting for your place with flour on your hands.

Trust & FAQ

Questions, Answered

Your photos, in plain terms: a recipe photo you pick or that UlamHub pulls from an imported link syncs to your account's cloud storage so it's there on all your devices. A photo or text you submit to an AI tool — Kitchen AI, Leftover Alchemist, Ingredient Scanner, What Can I Make?, or a scanned-photo import — is separately sent to OpenAI to generate that result. Full detail in the Privacy Policy.

What is UlamHub?+
UlamHub is a recipe app rooted in Filipino home cooking and built for every cuisine. You can save recipes from anywhere, get help from an AI sous-chef, plan your week, and shop with a grocery list that builds itself.
What cuisines can I cook with UlamHub?+
Filipino, Italian, American, Mexican, Japanese, Indian, Chinese, Greek, Thai, Korean, Vietnamese, French, Spanish and Lebanese are all built into Explore today — browse by cuisine or search for a specific dish or ingredient.
How do I add a recipe I found online or on social media?+
The fast way: right from the post or video, tap Share and choose UlamHub — the app opens straight into the import, link already in hand. Or do it manually: from Add Recipe, choose Import from link and paste a URL from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or a blog. Either way, AI reads the content and builds a clean recipe card you can review and save — and if the source has its own photo, UlamHub pulls that in too.
What exactly happens to my photos and text when I use an AI feature?+
It's sent to OpenAI, which generates the recipe, suggestion, or answer and sends it back — we don't use it to train any model. This is separate from a recipe's own photo (one you pick, or one pulled from an imported link), which stays tied to that recipe in your account's cloud storage rather than being sent anywhere else. See the Privacy Policy for the full breakdown by feature.
Can I trust the nutrition numbers and AI-suggested dishes?+
Treat them as a helpful estimate, not verified medical or dietary advice. AI can make mistakes — including about ingredient substitutions, quantities, and allergens — so always use your own judgment before cooking or serving anything it suggests, especially around food safety and allergies.
Can UlamHub read a photo of a handwritten recipe?+
Yes — choose Scan a photo from Add Recipe and AI reads a handwritten or printed recipe straight from the photo.
What can Kitchen AI actually do?+
Kitchen AI is a chat assistant inside the app — ask it what to cook with what you have, have it plan your week to a budget, or ask it a cooking question, by text or voice.
Does UlamHub build a grocery list for me?+
It builds the list you ask it to. Tap Add to grocery list on any day in your Meal Planner, and that day's ingredients merge in — the same ingredient from multiple days combines into one line instead of listing twice. Nothing gets added automatically. Each item has a Substitute button, and Cooking Abroad mode adds substitutes plus the nearest specialty store.
Can I cook with family or share my recipes with someone else?+
Yes — joining and browsing a Shared Kitchen is free for everyone. With UlamHub Premium, you can host your own kitchen and hand out an invite code; anyone who joins (free or Premium) can browse what's there, but actually adding a recipe to your own cookbook — yours or theirs — needs Premium and the owner's approval, request by request.
What's free, and what needs Premium?+
Free covers saving up to 10 of your own recipes, meal planning, your grocery list, and joining/browsing a Shared Kitchen — no time limit, no trial clock. Premium ($4.99/mo or $39.99/yr) removes the recipe cap and unlocks every AI tool, Recipe Story, hosting a Shared Kitchen and adding recipes from one, and community trending. See the full breakdown above in Pricing.
Is UlamHub available on iOS and Android?+
UlamHub is built for both and is currently in final testing — join the early access list above and we'll let you know the moment it's live.
Is UlamHub available in Spanish?+
Yes — open Profile → Preferences → Language to switch the whole app between English and Español. Cuisine names and recipe content stay as originally written so nothing gets lost in translation.
